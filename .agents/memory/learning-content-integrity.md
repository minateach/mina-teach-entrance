---
name: Complete learning content and truthful audits
description: Preserve all available topic materials and distinguish delivery integrity from full textbook coverage.
---

Do not replace available topic material with summaries or selected excerpts. Long
material may use numbered sections, but every section must remain reachable and
load failures must remain visible rather than being treated as successful completion.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires all available notes, subtopics, definitions,
examples, formulas, diagrams, tables, activities, questions, answers and resources,
and rejects claims that missing curriculum content is complete.

**How to apply:** Check stored content against what the backend delivers and what
the reader exposes. A successful audit of registered records is not proof that all
uploaded PDFs or external textbooks have been imported. Report source-mapping and
ingestion gaps separately.

Completion refers to the material actually accessed, not a permanent claim about a
lesson regardless of later additions. Preserve saves and bookmarks across meaningful
content revisions without automatically counting newly added material as studied.

**Why:** Retaining a saved lesson and retaining completion of unseen learning material
are different behaviors; accurate progress requires that distinction.

**How to apply:** Keep lesson identity stable for reopening while associating
completion with the content revision the learner accessed.

Education-system repairs must cover the subject → grade → chapter → topic →
practice journey, not only the Practice button.

**Why:** The user repeatedly requested that every subject, grade, topic and
navigation control work, and explicitly rejected fixing only one button.

**How to apply:** Exercise individual links and controls on desktop and mobile.
Keep missing curriculum or question records separate from broken navigation;
neither a successful page load nor a missing-content notice proves a usable course.

A saved practice attempt must not override an explicitly requested grade, subject
or topic. Offer a separate, deliberate resume action when its scope differs or
cannot be established.

**Why:** Automatic restoration of an unrelated unfinished session can make a correct
subject link appear to open another subject.

**How to apply:** Preserve the previous resume state until the learner explicitly
resumes it or agrees to replace it. Keep the return destination consistent with
the lesson or subject actually being practised.
