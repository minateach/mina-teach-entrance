# Content rating and audience notes

These are observations for the owner to use while completing Play Console's official questionnaire. They are not a rating result.

- Product purpose: secondary-school education and entrance-exam practice.
- User-to-user communication: no public student-to-student messaging was found.
- AI-generated content: students can submit text and receive AI-generated educational or general answers; web research and submitted images may be processed by external providers.
- User-submitted media: students may submit a photo to the AI Assistant or use Image Studio; the app does not publish those submissions to other students.
- Ads and purchases: no advertising or in-app billing integration is present in the Android wrapper configuration.
- Permissions: wrapper is configured without location, microphone, or notification delegation. Camera/photo selection is initiated by the user through the web page.
- Audience: owner must confirm the intended age groups and any family/child-directed declarations in Play Console. Do not guess or mark the app as child-directed without an owner decision.
- Rating: complete the official Play Console questionnaire against the final production build; no specific rating is asserted here.
