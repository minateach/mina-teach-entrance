## Full description

MINA TEACH AI helps Ethiopian secondary-school students study and prepare for entrance examinations.

- Browse available learning materials for Grades 9–12.
- Practice with available reviewed questions and exams.
- Ask the AI Assistant for study explanations in English, Afaan Oromoo, or Amharic.
- Save supported learning progress and use downloaded lessons offline.
- Use image-study tools when an online AI service is available.

Available lessons and reviewed questions vary by subject and grade. The app does not claim that every Ethiopian textbook or curriculum unit is complete or officially approved. AI responses are generated assistance, not official Ministry of Education content or official examination answers.

The AI Assistant, current web research, and AI image features require internet access and may send the submitted request to the configured AI provider. See the privacy policy before use.
