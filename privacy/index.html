Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This draft describes the current MINA TEACH AI application. It is provided for review and is not a claim of legal or Google Play policy compliance.

Information the app handles

  • Account details: when you create an account, Clerk handles your sign-in identity, including your email address and any profile name you choose.
  • Study activity: when you use a signed-in account, the app can save conversations, learning progress, preferences, exam attempts, and saved-question bookmarks. Some study data and downloaded lessons also remain in your browser or on your device for offline use.
  • Content you submit: questions and text sent to the AI Assistant, and images you explicitly submit for analysis or editing. Images used by the assistant are kept in this browser for conversation continuity; they are sent to an AI provider only when you submit the request. Image Studio inputs are sent only when you request an AI edit or generation.
  • Technical safeguards: the service records limited provider-usage counts and request limits. The current app does not include Google Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

How information is used and shared

MINA TEACH AI uses submitted text and images to provide the requested learning or image features. General AI chat is sent through the configured MiniMax service. Current web research and submitted-image analysis may be sent to Google Gemini; when web grounding is used, the search request is handled by Google's search grounding service. AI image editing or generation uses the configured MiniMax service. These providers process request content under their own terms and privacy practices; MINA TEACH AI does not claim that providers retain no request data.

Signed-in account records are stored in the application's PostgreSQL database and scoped to the account. Browser-only learning and offline records stay on the device unless you choose to synchronize supported records by signing in. The app does not sell personal information or use advertising SDKs.

Permissions and device storage

The Android distribution is planned as a Trusted Web Activity that opens the existing website. The application does not request location or microphone access. The browser may open the camera or photo picker only when you choose a photo/camera control. Offline lessons, progress, and image attachments use browser storage on your device.

Retention and deletion

You can use the Account page to download or delete the Mina Teach account records covered by its current data controls. Deleting those saved records does not delete the Clerk sign-in account. Exam attempts and saved-question bookmarks are stored separately and are not currently removed by that saved-record deletion control. Browser-only data can be cleared from the device; uninstalling or clearing browser storage may remove it.

To request deletion of your sign-in account or ask about deletion of separately stored records, email minaseyfufa@gmail.com from the account address. A verified deletion process and its time limits still require owner review before store submission.

Children and students

The app is designed for secondary-school study. It does not ask for a student's precise location. Students should not enter passwords, sensitive personal details, or unrelated private information into AI prompts or uploaded materials. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app's data behavior changes. Questions or deletion requests: minaseyfufa@gmail.com.

Owner review needed: confirm the provider disclosures, data retention, account-deletion process, and contact details before relying on this page as a final store policy.
