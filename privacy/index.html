Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This plain-language page describes the current MINA TEACH AI app behavior. It is an owner-review draft, not legal advice or a claim of regulatory or Google Play compliance.

Information the app handles

  • Account details: Clerk handles the app sign-in identity, including your email address and profile name. If you choose to link Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication also processes that Google identity. A Firebase UID is linked only while you are signed in to your existing Mina Teach account; accounts are not merged by email.
  • Study records: signed-in conversations, learning progress, and preferences are stored in Replit PostgreSQL. Online exam attempts and saved exam-question bookmarks are stored separately in that database. Some settings, progress, downloaded lessons and question sets, offline results, bookmarks, and image attachments are also held in browser storage on your device.
  • Content you submit: the AI Assistant sends your question and relevant conversation context to the selected AI provider when you submit it. Images attached to assistant conversations may remain in browser storage for continuity; when submitted, the image is sent to the provider used for that request. Image Studio sends an image only when you request an AI edit or generation.
  • Service operation: the app stores limited aggregate AI and search usage counts to enforce request limits. Browser notification permission is managed by your browser. Signed-in notification preferences are stored with account preferences; new-content checks run only while the Learning Dashboard is open.

How information is used and shared

General AI chat is ordinarily sent through the configured MiniMax service; if the configured Gemini fallback is used, the request is sent to Google Gemini instead. Requests for current web research and submitted-image analysis may also be sent to Gemini. Google Search grounding is used only when it actually runs for a request. AI image edits or generation use the configured MiniMax service. These providers process submitted content under their own terms and privacy practices. MINA TEACH AI does not claim that providers retain no request data.

Replit hosts the app and its PostgreSQL-backed account records. Clerk remains the app sign-in provider. If you explicitly link Google Sign-In from Account, Firebase Authentication stores that linked identity. If you choose “Save private Firestore copy,” Firebase stores a copy of conversations, learning records, and preferences under rules that restrict access to the signed-in Firebase UID. That copy is not AI model training and excludes online exam history, question bookmarks, and private Knowledge Center files.

The current app does not send analytics events and does not include Firebase Crashlytics or advertising SDKs. Firebase Analytics and Cloud Messaging are not reported as active until their web configuration is valid and tested. This statement describes the app implementation, not provider-side data practices.

Browser-only records stay on that device unless a supported feature synchronizes them to your signed-in account. Clearing site storage or uninstalling the Android wrapper can remove device-only records and downloads.

Permissions and device storage

The Android package is a Trusted Web Activity that opens the existing website. The app does not request location or microphone access. The browser opens a camera or photo picker only when you choose an image control. Offline lessons, progress, question sets, results, and image attachments use browser storage. Background push notifications are not configured; reminders and content checks run only while the relevant page is open.

Review, download, and deletion controls

Use Account to import browser conversations and learning data, download a JSON copy, or delete your Mina Teach account. The download contains your profile name and email plus conversations, learning records, and preferences; it does not include online exam attempts, question bookmarks, private Knowledge Center files, device-only downloads, or local image attachments.

Account deletion removes the app’s saved records, online exam attempts, question bookmarks, private Knowledge Center files, the Clerk sign-in identity, and this browser’s supported Mina Teach data. If Google Sign-In is linked, the deletion flow asks you to reauthenticate, then removes the Firestore copy and Firebase identity. The app reports when an identity or local file could not be confirmed as deleted; contact minaseyfufa@gmail.com for help finishing a failed deletion. This page does not promise a deletion time limit.

Local data is device-specific. Browser storage may be cleared by you, the browser, or operating-system storage management. Provider retention for AI requests is governed by the provider’s own terms; the app does not set or promise those providers’ retention periods.

Students

MINA TEACH AI is designed for secondary-school learning. Do not submit passwords, sensitive personal details, or unrelated private information in AI prompts or uploaded materials. A parent or guardian can contact us with privacy questions.

Changes and contact

This page may change when the app’s data behavior changes. Questions or deletion requests: minaseyfufa@gmail.com.
