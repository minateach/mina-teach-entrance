Terms of Use

Last updated: 22 November 2026

These plain-language terms describe the current MINA TEACH AI service. They are an owner-review draft, not legal advice or a claim of regulatory or store compliance.

Using the learning service

MINA TEACH AI provides learning pages, practice activities, study planning, and AI-assisted explanations for educational use. Use the app respectfully and lawfully. Keep your sign-in details private, and do not submit passwords, sensitive personal information, or other people’s private information in messages or uploads.

AI and learning content

AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect. Check important answers against your textbook, teacher, or another reliable source. AI-generated explanations and practice are not official Ethiopian Ministry of Education material or official examination questions unless the app identifies a verifiable source. The app does not guarantee a learning or examination result.

Images, web research, and external services

Submitting a question, image, web-research request, or image-edit request sends the relevant content to the provider needed for that feature. General AI chat and image editing use the configured MiniMax service. Image analysis and web research may use Google Gemini; Google Search is used only when it actually runs for a request. Provider terms and privacy practices apply to their processing. Read the Privacy Policy for current data handling details.

Accounts and saved information

Signed-in accounts can save conversations, learning records, and preferences. The Account page offers a JSON download and a permanent deletion flow for online records, private Knowledge Center files, the Clerk sign-in identity, and supported device data. If Google is linked, deletion also requires Google reauthentication to remove the Firebase identity and Firestore copy. The app reports if an identity or local file could not be confirmed as deleted. See the Account page and Privacy Policy for the current scope.

Offline features

Downloaded lessons, question sets, progress, and some results are stored in browser storage on the device. Device storage can be cleared or lost, and offline records may not synchronize until the app reconnects and the account is confirmed. The AI Assistant and online account features require internet access. Background push delivery is not configured; reminders and content checks run while the relevant page is open.

Service changes and availability

Features, available content, and external provider access may change. The app may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance or provider outages. No statement on this page changes the provider’s own terms or promises a particular recovery or data-retention window.
